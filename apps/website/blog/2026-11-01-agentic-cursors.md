Intro: from chat window to canvas. Why linear chat is limiting and why a spatial workspace suits agents.

Over the course of the last 3 years all of us got pretty used to typing questions, concerns or commands into chatboxes usually placed at the center, the bottom left or the bottom right of the screen. We got used to treating AI as a friend or a colleague that we quickly can ask for a solution and ping-pong different ideas back and forth with in order to derive a conclusion.

As a software developer most of us probably have noticed a shift, starting back in late 2025 where AI models got capable enough so that one could describe a task and hand it off to a single agent, that would go navigate through the codebase and eventually would solve the requested task.

Fast forward to late 2026 this single agent increasingly more often has become 5 concurrent agents all working on the codebase in the cloud in order to chuck away ticket after ticket.

I noticed that it gets more and more difficult to simultaneously handle 5 agents at the same time. I often find myself switching back and forth between different conversations, checking screenshots, reading responses and mixing up chat windows. While chat has worked very well in the single agent era, it seems like it is not the right medium for concurrent multi agent work.
